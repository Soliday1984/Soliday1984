## [DogDanceAI](https://dogdanceai.com/)

Turn a dog or cat photo into a short AI dancing video for TikTok, Instagram Reels, or YouTube Shorts.

- Upload one clear pet photo in your browser.
- Get an approximately 4-second, silent, vertical MP4, usually in about five minutes.
- Add music in your social app when posting.
- Pay once: $9.99 for one video or $19.99 for three, with no subscription.
- Failed videos are refunded automatically.
